We start by showing you
what your day actually does

Opta does not arrive as a new workflow. It arrives as a mirror.

One unit. It starts in a single department, chosen with you. Nothing changes anywhere else.

Weeks of watching. For the first weeks Opta changes nothing and measures everything: where the day actually went, where it waited, how long each handoff took. You see your own numbers before anyone acts on a recommendation.

Then, one step at a time. When the picture matches what your people know to be true, Opta starts to act on it, one kind of decision at a time, each one recorded and settled against the outcome. You decide what it takes on next, and when.

What you need to bring. The systems you already run and a department head who wants the numbers. Opta supplies the rest.

Nobody has to trust Opta on the first day.  They only have to look.
You do not have to take our word for it; that is the design principle.

Introducing Opta

Opta is a layer that sits alongside the system of record and adds operational control to improve the care experience for patients and providers. It delivers measurable improvement in operational outcomes.

It works with the systems hospitals already run, and it never replaces a clinician’s judgment.

Opta sees what’s ahead and
helps the team act in time

Opta maintains a live picture of the floor, sees potential problems and opportunities as they develop, calculates appropriate responses, surfaces them to the right clinician in time to act, and logs the result for transparency.

The result is a better care experience for patients, time for staff to act, and measurably improved operating metrics for hospitals.

Built on deep
domain expertise

Opta’s team includes a board-certified emergency physician with twenty-eight years’ experience and an AI-native systems architect who’s spent the last sixteen years building closed-loop control systems.

The result is a control layer with thirty-three patents pending that lets clinicians see what’s ahead to deliver a better care experience.

What Opta does

Opta maintains a live picture of the entire physical operation — every room, team, and piece of equipment — sees delays while they are still forming, and acts in time to prevent them. It runs alongside the hospital’s record system, and it stays silent when acting would not help. Every recovery it claims is measured on the hospital’s own records, not estimated on ours.

AI, applied safely

Applying AI safely is now a global priority. Opta is an architecture for doing it.

Opta is a model for how to use AI safely and responsibly to make life better. It was built with AI, and is better for it. It runs on an architecture that is safer than an AI agent by construction: intelligence proposes, and nothing acts on its own reasoning. And it runs without a frontier model at runtime, so the operation it serves adds nothing to the compute footprint the world is now weighing.

Hospitals are where we are proving it, because a hospital is the one place an AI system acting on its own reasoning is not an acceptable risk. The architecture is not specific to healthcare.
